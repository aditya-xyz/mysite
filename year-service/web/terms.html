Terms of use

A few ground rules for a little book of memories.

The Pixelbook admins share Pixelbook with invited friends and family. These terms describe access to this private scrapbook and its optional gifts. Please also read our Privacy Policy.

Keep your invitation private

Use only the invitation and photos shared with your household. Please do not forward invitation links or verification codes, try to enter another household's scrapbook, bypass download limits, or interfere with the service. We may revoke an invitation or take an edition offline.

Be thoughtful with photos

You can view the scrapbook and use the download, sharing and gift options made available to you. Access to a photo does not transfer its copyright or grant permission for commercial use. Before reposting or sharing photos outside your household, check with the photographer and the people pictured. Please do not publicly publish private addresses, contact details or invitation links.

If a photo, caption or other item concerns you or you believe it infringes your rights, contact us in the conversation where you received your invitation. Identify the item so we can review it and remove or restrict it where appropriate.

Optional household gifts

Available gifts, eligible destinations and deadlines are shown in the scrapbook. Gifts are subject to the invitation's allowance, stock, supplier availability and our budget. They are not subscriptions or an entitlement to future gifts, and there are no recurring charges to you.

For eligible live orders, the limits are one postcard up to $10 and one mug up to $25 per household, including shipping and tax. The Pixelbook admins fund the offered gift. A request above the applicable limit is held rather than automatically purchased. A preview or saved request is not proof that a supplier has accepted a live order.

Check the recipient name, mailing address and selected design before confirming. Production, shipping and delivery depend on the supplier and carrier; mockups are illustrations and final placement or color can vary. If you need a correction or cancellation, contact us promptly. We cannot promise changes after a supplier begins fulfillment.

Address suggestions and verification

Google Maps Platform provides address suggestions and verification. Use of those features is subject to the Google Maps / Google Earth Additional Terms of Service, incorporated into these terms, and Google's Privacy Policy. You are responsible for confirming that the saved address is the correct delivery address.

Contact and removal

Contact the Pixelbook admins in the original invitation conversation for help with access, content, gifts or removal of your information. Our Privacy Policy explains retention and what removal can and cannot change once information has reached a supplier or recipient.
