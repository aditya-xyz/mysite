# Pixelbook winter frames v1

Picker order: **Starry Night** (default), **Evergreen**, **Winterlight**.
Opaque, blank frame backgrounds; photos, captions, signature and Pixel are drawn
separately by the application and are never regenerated as part of a frame.
Built-in imagegen edited the user-approved frame references on 2026-11-15.

Prompt set (common instructions): produce a flat portrait 4:5 reusable background;
remove all photographs, people, dog cutout, text, heart and collage dividers;
leave continuous blank warm ivory paper. No watermark or presentation background.
Keep the central rectangle x=8–92%, y=8–91% clear for app-rendered content.

- Starry Night: retain textured navy border, restrained scattered gold stars,
  starbursts, bottom tree silhouettes and low rolling navy hills.
- Evergreen: retain textured deep green border and small pinecone-and-needle
  clusters only at top right and bottom left, tucked away from the photo area.
- Winterlight: retain warm ivory paper and delicate pine sprigs/pinecones at
  top right and bottom left, with no dark border.

Files are versioned so saved exports and already-submitted proofs remain immutable.

Final workspace assets:
- `web/frames/starry-night-v1.png`
- `web/frames/evergreen-v1.png`
- `web/frames/winterlight-v1.png`

Screen-only previews (`*-preview-v1.webp`) are 720×900 WebP derivatives of these
approved backgrounds, encoded at quality 85. The picker preloads and decodes all
three; full-size PNGs remain the source for saved and printed artwork.
The bottom photo slots retain their original 544:480 proportions at 484×427,
with a smaller heading and the heart beside the signature to recover photo height.

## Winterlight v2 — winter vines and songbirds

Winterlight now uses `web/frames/winterlight-v2.png` and the screen-only
`web/frames/winterlight-preview-v2.webp` (720×900, WebP quality 85). The v1 assets
remain available for historical artwork. Starry Night remains default;
Evergreen stays second.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using Winterlight as the edit target
and Starry Night as a reference for airy composition with a richer lower edge.
Final prompt set: Replace pinecones with holly; loosen dense, uniform decoration
into asymmetric scattered accents and a winding lower vine with generous paper
gaps. Add two small winter songbirds, tucked into opposite outer margins. Shift
the decoration toward dusty blues, blue-gray, pearl-white berries and snowy leaf
accents, with some soft sage and only a few muted red berries. Preserve warm
ivory textured paper, delicate botanical watercolor, a richer airy bottom edge
and lighter irregular upper/side motifs. Keep the central rectangle x=8–92%,
y=8–92% blank; tuck decorations outward into the narrow outer bands. Portrait
4:5 opaque reusable frame. No dense garland, mirrored patterns, uniform rows,
pinecones, conifers, stars, photos, text, dogs, logos, or watermark.
