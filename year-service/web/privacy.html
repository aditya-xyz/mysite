Privacy at Pixelbook

A private scrapbook, shared with our people.

Aditya and Angela maintain Pixelbook for invited friends and family. This page explains how we use information to share the scrapbook, protect access, and arrange optional gifts.

We use your details for your invitation and gifts. Pixelbook has no advertising analytics or session replay. We keep saved addresses and gift-order records until you ask us to remove them; there is no automatic deletion date.

What we keep, and why

  • Invitations: names, phone numbers, household membership, language preferences and invitation settings, so the right people receive access to the right photos.
  • Scrapbook content: photos, captions, accessibility descriptions and the people they are shared with. Aditya and Angela curate this content.
  • Sign-in and security: invitation and session records, verification timestamps, request limits and photo-download counts. We use hashed network identifiers in application rate limits; our hosting providers also process connection information such as IP addresses.
  • Optional gifts: a confirmed mailing address, recipient name, photo or mug choices, artwork, price checks, supplier identifiers and fulfillment status. These let us prepare and deliver the gift you request and avoid duplicate orders.

Who helps run Pixelbook

  • Cloudflare hosts the application and stores our database and private media. Connection and service-security data is processed as part of hosting.
  • Twilio Verify receives the phone number used for requested sign-in codes and processes verification requests. These are access messages, not a marketing subscription.
  • Google Maps Platform helps suggest and verify mailing addresses. When suggestions are available, partial street-address text is sent through our server to Google as you type. Choosing a suggestion requests its address details; verifying sends the entered address to Google. You can type an address without choosing a suggestion, but using Verify still sends the address for checking.
  • Handwrytten and Printful receive the artwork needed to prepare card designs or mug mockups. If you place a live gift order, the relevant supplier also receives the recipient name, delivery address and order details. These services use their own systems, subprocessors and delivery partners to process and fulfill orders.

These services may process information in countries other than your own. Their processing is also described in their respective policies: Cloudflare, Twilio, Google, Handwrytten and Printful. Google's Privacy Policy is incorporated here for our use of Google Maps Platform.

Cookies, preferences and photos on your device

We use necessary cookies to remember your invitation and authenticated session. The invitation cookie lasts up to one day; a signed-in session lasts up to seven days, and may end sooner if your invitation expires or access is revoked. Signing out ends the current session.

Your browser can remember your color preference in local storage. Private display photos are temporarily cached in the open tab's memory, not saved by our photo cache to persistent browser storage. Photos you deliberately download or share can remain on your device or with the people you send them to.

How long we retain information

We keep saved contact details, confirmed addresses, scrapbook content and gift-order records until you request removal or we choose to remove them while maintaining the scrapbook. There is no automatic purge date for these records. Expired sign-in sessions and temporary verification data are cleaned up separately; expiry does not erase your saved address or order history.

Removing information from Pixelbook does not automatically remove a supplier's records, delivery records, copies already downloaded by recipients or infrastructure backups. We will explain any remaining records and help with a supplier removal request where possible. Some supplier records may need to be retained for fulfillment, accounting, security or legal obligations.

Questions, corrections or removal

Reply to Aditya or Angela in the original conversation where you received your invitation. You can ask us to correct your contact or address, remove a photo or caption, end access, or remove your saved details and gift records. We may need to confirm which invitation or records are yours before making changes.

Removing a saved address may prevent an unsubmitted gift from being delivered. If an order has already reached a supplier, contact us promptly; deleting a site record does not cancel a shipment.

Family access

Invitations and gift details are intended to be managed by adults. If a child would like to use a household invitation, please contact us first so we can agree on appropriate adult-managed access. Please tell us if a child's details need to be corrected or removed.
