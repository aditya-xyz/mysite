Privacy on this portfolio

This public site introduces my work and projects.

Visits and preferences

This version of the portfolio does not load Google Analytics, advertising trackers or session replay. Your browser may remember your chosen color palette in local storage.

Netlify hosts the site and processes ordinary connection and service-security information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect it. Its practices are described in Netlify's Privacy Statement. Removing the analytics tag from this site does not delete information already collected in the past.

Links to other services

Project and social links take you to other sites with their own privacy practices. Pixelbook is a separate, invitation-only scrapbook; its Privacy Policy describes its sign-in and gift features.

Questions or removal

Contact Aditya through LinkedIn if you have a privacy question or a removal request about this portfolio. That conversation is handled by LinkedIn under its own policies.
